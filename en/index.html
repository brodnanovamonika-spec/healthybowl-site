How it works

Three steps.
No science.

You open the app and you are done. Healthy Bowl does the rest.

1

Healthy Bowl philosophy
The middle path between a diet and eating purely for pleasure.

Healthy Bowl is an app-designed bowl built on balanced eating, the middle path between a diet of restriction and eating purely for pleasure. We treat food only as nourishment, so we neither over-flavour nor over-decorate it.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is the app free?
Yes. For now it is entirely free and you pay nothing.
Is this a diet or medical advice?
No. Healthy Bowl is an aid for everyday balanced eating based on general WHO guidance. It does not replace a doctor or a dietitian. If you have health restrictions, talk to a professional.
Where is my data stored?
Your settings, profiles and records are stored on your phone. Only what is necessary is sent to the server, for example a message you write in Support, or an address if you enter one to get prices for your region.
Does it work offline?
After the first load, yes, core features work offline. Things that need the server, such as current prices, update when you are online again.
Can I set allergies?
Yes. You mark allergens and intolerances, the app shows which allergens a bowl contains and offers a swap.
Which languages are available?
12 languages: Slovak, English, German, Hungarian, Swedish, Finnish, Russian, Ukrainian, Spanish, Portuguese, Czech and Polish. The language switches without a restart.
Will it be on Google Play?
We are working on it. Until then you use it as a web app straight from your phone's home screen.
